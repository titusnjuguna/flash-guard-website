1. Who we are

FlashGuard is provided by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [registered address] ("we", "us"). Contact: [privacy@yourdomain.com]. [Data protection officer / ODPC registration number, if applicable.]

2. The short version

  • The FlashGuard extension scans text inside your browser. The text you type, your prompts and AI replies are never sent to us.
  • When a threat is detected and redacted, only a small event is reported to your company's admin dashboard: the threat type (for example API_KEY), the page address, the time, and the extension version and browser. The secret itself is never included.
  • Events are not linked to a named employee.

3. What we collect

Extension events (from employees' browsers). Threat type, page address (domain and path, without query string or fragment; on some AI tools the path contains a conversation identifier), timestamp, extension version and browser name. The company key is sent with each event to identify the customer account.

Stored on the device. The company key and a short queue of unsent events, held in the browser's local extension storage.

Admin accounts and billing. Admin email address, company name, seat count and subscription status. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not receive or store full card numbers.

Website and waitlist. If you join the waitlist we collect your email, company, team size, browsers used and, if present, the campaign source in the link. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this site [update if that changes]. The dashboard stores a sign-in session in your browser.

4. How we use it

To provide the service and dashboard, authenticate admins, bill customers, prevent abuse, support customers, and send service and waitlist emails. We do not sell personal data or use event data for advertising.

5. Our role

For extension events, the customer (your employer) is the controller and we are the processor acting on its instructions; a data processing agreement is available on request. For admin accounts, billing and the waitlist, we are the controller. Customers are responsible for telling their employees that FlashGuard is deployed.
