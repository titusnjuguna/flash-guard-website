Frequently Asked Questions

Technical answers on cryptography, security guarantees, and deployment.

Does FlashGuard inspect or store the text of employee prompts?
No. FlashGuard operates on a strict zero-knowledge architecture. Detection regex evaluates prompts in volatile client-side browser memory. When a violation is caught, only high-level metadata (e.g. THREAT: STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and domain chatgpt.com) is pushed to your admin audit log. The secret itself and the prompt content never touch any external server.
How does FlashGuard prevent employees from uninstalling the extension?
When deployed via Google Workspace Admin (for Chrome), Microsoft Intune (for Edge), or Group Policy, the extension is set to force_installed. Under enterprise browser management policies, end-users cannot remove, disable, or toggle the extension in normal or incognito browsing.
How does Paystack billing work with no recurring subscriptions?
We believe in honest, predictable billing without sneaky auto-debits. You pay once for your chosen billing term (1 month or 1 year) via Paystack. Your company console remains active for the duration. When renewal approaches, your admin can top-up, renew, or adjust seats with one click.
What happens if a developer needs to share a false-positive token?
FlashGuard provides a one-click "Redact Secret" button that replaces the sensitive token with a sanitized mock placeholder (e.g. [REDACTED_STRIPE_KEY]), allowing the AI assistant to debug the syntax or logic safely without the real production key ever leaving the machine.
